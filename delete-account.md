# Delete your FittArc account and data

_Last updated: October 2026_

You can delete your FittArc account and everything stored for it at any time.

## In the app (fastest)

1. Open FittArc and go to **Profile**.
2. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

This permanently deletes your sign-in, your backed-up progress (daily logs, profile, badges, levels and streaks), your friend code, your friend requests and friend list, and the challenges you created. It also clears all FittArc data on your phone. If you signed in a long time ago, the app may ask you to sign in again before it deletes the account.

## By email

If you can't open the app, write to **support@fittarc.in** from the email address of your Google account and ask us to delete your account. We will delete it and reply once it is done, usually within 30 days.

## What stays

Nothing is kept after deletion, except what the law requires us to keep. Health readings you typed in, heart-rate estimates, watch data and photos only ever lived on your phone, so uninstalling the app removes them.

Questions: support@fittarc.in. See also our [privacy policy](/privacy/).
